## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Premise 1 (Observation 1)**: In `currency_utils.dart`, a leading `+` sign is stripped without updating `isNegative` or tracking that a sign was already seen (`hasLeadingSign` is missing).
2. **Premise 2 (Observation 1)**: Inside `if (stripCurrencySymbols)`, line 55 checks `if (!isNegative && trimmed.startsWith('-'))`. Because `isNegative` was left `false`, the check succeeds on any trailing minus sign (e.g. in `"+-10"` or `"+¥-10"`).
3. **Step 3 (Defect Impact)**: This causes conflicting signs (`+-10` and `+¥-10`) to silently strip both signs and assign `isNegative = true`, returning `-1000` rather than throwing `FormatException`.
4. **Step 4 (Remediation)**: By adding `bool hasLeadingSign = false;` (or `hasExplicitSign`), we set `hasLeadingSign = true` upon consuming either `+` or `-`. In the second pass (after currency symbol stripping), we guard sign extraction with `if (!hasLeadingSign)`.
5. **Step 5 (Validation & Verification, Observation 2)**: Any second sign remains unstripped in `trimmed`. Downstream `RegExp(r'^\d+$')` integer validation guarantees that unstripped signs trigger a `FormatException`. In addition, `tryParseToMinorUnits` catches the exception and returns `null`.
6. **Conclusion**: The proposed sign parsing logic completely closes the vulnerability across all conflicting sign patterns (`+-10`, `-+10`, `++10`, `--10`, `+¥-10`, `-¥+10`, `-¥-10`, `+¥+10`, `¥+-10`, etc.) while properly supporting valid combinations (`¥-10`, `¥+10`, `+¥10`, `-¥10`).

## 3. Caveats

- **Fullwidth Signs**: The parser operates on standard ASCII `+` and `-`. Standard OCR pipelines (Vision and ML Kit) normalize ASCII signs prior to number extraction. Fullwidth signs (e.g. `＋`, `－`) are not part of standard ASCII numeric literals.
- **Read-only Investigation**: In accordance with the system constraints, no direct modifications have been made to `lib/core/utils/currency_utils.dart`. The full drop-in patch is documented in `report.md` for application by the remediation worker.
